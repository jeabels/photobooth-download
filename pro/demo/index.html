Prints go straight to this printer with no dialog. DNP: in Windows → Printers → DNP → Printing preferences, choose the same paper (PC 4×6 for 4×6 and 2×6), and for 2×6 strips turn on 2 inch cut: each 4×6 sheet then gives two strips. Fine scatters tiny dots for the sharpest detail and hair. Smooth uses an even dot pattern for softer skin and cleaner backgrounds. Silk (recommended) smooths out camera grain, keeps faces clear and prints a fine, even texture, like a classic receipt photobooth.
