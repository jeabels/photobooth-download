Real photo printers

DNP (DS620, RX1, QW410), Epson and Canon Selphy with no print dialog. 2×6 strips print two per 4×6 sheet with the DNP cutter.

The colour strip and the Boomerang on a download page. Guests scan before they even leave the booth.

Video booth
