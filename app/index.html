Everything a paid photobooth needs, already set up.

Built for Filipino photobooth businesses: pay-per-print at malls and pop-ups, or hourly rentals for weddings and parties.

Automatic GCash

The guest scans your GCash QR. The moment the money lands, the camera starts — no "I've paid" button, no checking phones.

Prints on what you own

Bluetooth receipt printers (58 or 80 mm) or any colour photo printer Android can print to, on 2×6, 4×6, 5×7 or A6 paper.

Digital copy by QR

Every strip gets a QR code. Guests scan it and save the photo to their phone, in colour or black and white.

Looks like your brand

Seven themes, your own header and fonts, five start-button styles, and a photo or live-video wallpaper.

Stickers, text and designs

Guests decorate each shot. You upload your own frame designs and place the photo spaces by hand.

Sales and event mode

Daily, weekly and monthly sales by cash and GCash, CSV export, and a timed mode for rented events.

What you need

An Android tabletAndroid 8 or newer, any size. The booth fills the screen automatically.
