Texture

Fine scatters tiny dots for the sharpest detail and hair. Smooth uses an even dot pattern for softer skin and cleaner backgrounds. Silk (recommended) smooths out camera grain, keeps faces clear and prints a fine, even texture, like a classic receipt photobooth.

Print speed

Steady ★ (recommended) prints at a normal pace with no lines over Bluetooth: the printer slows its head a little so Bluetooth always keeps ahead of it, and it never stops mid-photo. Full detail, normal darkness. If your printer still shows lines, tap Print speed test: it prints a grey sample at each speed. Pick the one without lines. A USB cable never has lines.

Top is the tone curve, bottom is the real dot pattern. Lower the darkness if faces print as black blobs.
